5 Launch

Mints the token (authorities renounced), creates every pool at your starting market cap, deposits each share single-sided, and permanently locks each position. Progress is journaled; if anything dies, come back with the same wallet and press Launch again — finished pools are skipped. Every transaction is real: there is no demo for this page.

    6 Send everything to your wallet

    Locked positions still earn fees. They move to your wallet with Orca's transfer_locked_position, then the un-pooled tokens and leftover SOL are swept.
